## ArogyaMitra Brand Philosophy
ArogyaMitra is a premium AI-powered health and wellness ecosystem. The design should feel natural, calming, and highly intelligent.

## Glassmorphism & Depth
Use translucent backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with heavy backdrop blurs (`blur(16px)`) to create depth over the deep forest background.

## Color Usage
- **Forest Green (#289c6e):** Used for primary actions, success states, and the core AI personality.
- **Saffron Amber (#f59e0b):** Used for warnings, energetic elements (workouts), and secondary accents.
